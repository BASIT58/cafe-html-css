About Us

We are a small cafe that serves delicious coffee and pastries. Our mission is to provide a cozy and welcoming environment where our customers can enjoy their favorite drinks and treats. Come visit us and experience the warmth of our cafe!

Our menu features a variety of coffee drinks, including espresso, cappuccino, latte, and more. We also offer a selection of teas and hot chocolate for those who prefer non-coffee options. Our pastries are freshly baked daily and include croissants, muffins, scones, and cookies.

We take pride in using high-quality ingredients and sourcing our coffee beans from sustainable farms. Our baristas are trained to craft each drink with care and attention to detail, so every cup is a perfect experience. We also offer a loyalty program for our regular customers.

Menu Specialties

Matcha Latte

Ingredients:

  • Matcha green tea powder
  • Hot water (not boiling)
  • Warm milk
  • Honey or maple syrup (optional)

Preparation Steps:

  1. Sift 1-2 teaspoons of matcha powder into a bowl to remove any lumps.
  2. Add a small amount of hot water (about 70-80°C) to the matcha powder.
  3. Whisk vigorously in a "W" pattern until frothy.
  4. Pour warm milk into your cup and serve.

Chocolate Milkshake

Ingredients:

  • 2 scoops of vanilla ice cream
  • 1 cup of cold whole milk
  • 2 tablespoons of chocolate syrup
  • Whipped cream
  • Chocolate shavings (optional)

Preparation Steps:

  1. Add the milk, ice cream, and chocolate syrup to a blender.
  2. Blend for 30-45 seconds until smooth and creamy.
  3. Pour into a tall glass.
  4. Top with whipped cream and chocolate shavings, then serve.

Fresh Strawberry Mocktail

Ingredients:

  • 6 fresh strawberries
  • 8 fresh mint leaves
  • 1 tablespoon of sugar or honey
  • Juice of half a lime
  • Ice cubes
  • Chilled soda water

Preparation Steps:

  1. Muddle the strawberries, mint, sweetener, and lime juice in a glass.
  2. Fill the glass with ice cubes.
  3. Top with chilled soda water.
  4. Stir gently and garnish with a mint leaf.

Classic Espresso

Ingredients:

  • 18 g of finely ground espresso beans
  • Hot filtered water (about 93°C)

Preparation Steps:

  1. Grind the beans finely and place them in the portafilter.
  2. Level and tamp the grounds firmly and evenly.
  3. Brew for 25-30 seconds until you get about 36 g of espresso.
  4. Serve immediately in a warmed cup.

Cappuccino

Ingredients:

  • 1 shot of espresso (about 30 ml)
  • 120 ml of cold milk
  • Cocoa powder (optional)

Preparation Steps:

  1. Brew the espresso directly into your cup.
  2. Steam the milk to about 60-65°C until thick and velvety.
  3. Pour the steamed milk over the espresso, then spoon the foam on top.
  4. Dust with cocoa powder if you like, and serve.

Iced Latte

Ingredients:

  • 2 shots of espresso (about 60 ml)
  • 180 ml of cold milk
  • Ice cubes
  • Vanilla or caramel syrup (optional)

Preparation Steps:

  1. Brew a double shot of espresso and let it cool for a minute.
  2. Fill a tall glass with ice cubes.
  3. Pour in the cold milk, then add syrup if you like.
  4. Pour the espresso over the top and stir gently.
