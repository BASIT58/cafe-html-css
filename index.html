About Us

We are a small cafe that serves delicious coffee and pastries. Our mission is to provide a cozy and welcoming environment for our customers to enjoy their favorite drinks and treats. Come visit us and experience the warmth of our cafe!

Our menu features a variety of coffee drinks, including espresso, cappuccino, latte, and more. We also offer a selection of teas and hot chocolate for those who prefer non-coffee options. Our pastries are freshly baked daily and include croissants, muffins, scones, and cookies.

We take pride in using high-quality ingredients and sourcing our coffee beans from sustainable farms. Our baristas are trained to craft each drink with care and attention to detail, ensuring that every cup is a perfect experience for our customers. We also offer a loyalty program for our regular customers.

Menu Specialties

Matcha Latte

Ingredients:

  • Matcha green tea powder
  • Hot water (not boiling)
  • Warm milk
  • Honey or maple syrup (optional)

Preparation Steps:

  1. Sift 1-2 teaspoons of matcha powder into a bowl to remove any lumps.
  2. Add a small amount of hot water (about 70-80°C) to the matcha powder.
  3. Whisk vigorously in a "W" pattern until frothy.
  4. Pour warm milk into your cup and serve.
